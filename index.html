Common Questions

Straight answers.

No jargon, no runaround. Here's what merchants ask before signing up.

"What if Bitcoin price drops after I receive it?"

You hold it like any other asset. When you want to convert, we put you in front of exchange partners who can do it properly and quickly.

"How does accounting and tax work?"

We connect you with accountants and auditors who already deal with this in HK. You're not figuring it out from scratch.

"Is this complicated for my staff?"

Customer scans the QR on the BASIC device. You see a confirmation. Market vendors have picked it up in under ten minutes.

"Do I need a license?"

No. Accepting Bitcoin for goods and services doesn't require an SFC license. The pilot is set up to be compliant from the start.
